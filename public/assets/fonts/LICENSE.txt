Self-hosted webfonts
====================

All three families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1,
which permits bundling and self-hosting.

  fraunces.woff2          Fraunces           © Undercase Type (Phaedra Charles, Flavia Zimbardi)
  instrument-sans.woff2   Instrument Sans    © Instrument
  jetbrains-mono.woff2    JetBrains Mono     © JetBrains s.r.o.

Files are the latin subsets served by Google Fonts, converted to woff2 variable
fonts where the family provides one:

  Fraunces          variable — optical size 9..144, weight 400..600
  Instrument Sans   variable — weight 400..600
  JetBrains Mono    static   — weight 400
